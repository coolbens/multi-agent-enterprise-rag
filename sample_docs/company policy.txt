ACME TECHNOLOGY SOLUTIONS
EMPLOYEE DATA SECURITY & AI USAGE POLICY

Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

------------------------------------------------------------
1. PURPOSE
------------------------------------------------------------

The purpose of this policy is to establish rules and procedures
for handling company data, confidential information, and the
use of Artificial Intelligence systems within ACME Technology
Solutions.

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants,
interns, and third-party vendors who access company systems,
documents, or AI platforms.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
------------------------------------------------------------

Employees must protect all confidential information including:

- Customer data
- Internal financial reports
- Source code
- Technical documentation
- HR records
- Product roadmaps
- Legal contracts
- Company manuals
- Security credentials

Confidential data must not be:

- Shared externally without authorization
- Uploaded to unauthorized AI platforms
- Copied to personal devices
- Stored in unsecured cloud services

------------------------------------------------------------
3. ACCEPTABLE AI USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Approved AI systems may be used for:

- Document summarization
- Internal knowledge retrieval
- Coding assistance
- Report generation
- Customer support automation
- Enterprise search systems

Employees must verify all AI-generated outputs before use.

AI-generated information must not be treated as guaranteed
accurate without human review.

------------------------------------------------------------
4. PROHIBITED AI USAGE
------------------------------------------------------------

Employees are prohibited from:

- Uploading sensitive customer data to public AI systems
- Generating malicious code
- Using AI for harassment or discrimination
- Bypassing security protocols using AI tools
- Sharing confidential prompts externally

Violation of these rules may result in disciplinary action,
including termination.

------------------------------------------------------------
5. PASSWORD POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

All employees must:

- Use strong passwords
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA)
- Change passwords every 90 days
- Never share credentials with other users

Minimum password requirements:

- At least 12 characters
- Uppercase and lowercase letters
- Numbers
- Special characters

------------------------------------------------------------
6. DATA RETENTION POLICY
------------------------------------------------------------

Company documents must follow retention schedules:

- HR records: 7 years
- Financial documents: 10 years
- Security logs: 2 years
- AI interaction logs: 1 year

Expired records must be securely deleted.

------------------------------------------------------------
7. REMOTE WORK SECURITY
------------------------------------------------------------

Remote employees must:

- Use company-approved VPN
- Lock devices when unattended
- Avoid public Wi-Fi without VPN
- Store company devices securely

------------------------------------------------------------
8. INCIDENT REPORTING
------------------------------------------------------------

Employees must immediately report:

- Data breaches
- Lost devices
- Suspicious login attempts
- Unauthorized AI usage
- Malware infections

Reports should be sent to:

security@acmetech.example

------------------------------------------------------------
9. ACCESS CONTROL
------------------------------------------------------------

Access to systems and documents is based on:

- Job responsibilities
- Role permissions
- Least-privilege principle

Unauthorized access attempts are logged and monitored.

------------------------------------------------------------
10. COMPLIANCE
------------------------------------------------------------

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:

- Suspension of system access
- Disciplinary action
- Legal consequences
- Employment termination

------------------------------------------------------------
11. ACKNOWLEDGMENT
------------------------------------------------------------

All employees must acknowledge that they have read,
understood, and agreed to follow this policy.
